01 — Agency Statement

Second Wind Pro was built on a singular premise: the landscape has changed, but the standard of elite representation has not.

We sit at the intersection of cultural influence and commercial execution—protecting the athlete, scaling their market value, and managing their transition through high-school recruitment, the transfer portal, and the professional arena.

A new generation of athletes deserves an agency built for the way the modern game is actually played—on the field, in the boardroom, and across every channel where attention lives.
